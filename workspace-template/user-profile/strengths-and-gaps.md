## How to frame gaps

Use:

`My strongest depth is in [X], and I have practical experience with [Y] in contexts such as [evidence].`

Avoid:

`I am an expert in [Y]` unless the profile clearly supports it.

## Stretch role handling

For stretch roles, always include:

- why the candidate could still succeed
- what would need ramp-up
- how to discuss the gap honestly
- whether the role is worth pursuing
