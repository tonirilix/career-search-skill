# Workflow: Recruiter Reply

## Goal

Draft natural, professional recruiter messages that move the process forward without revealing unnecessary leverage or anxiety.

## Inputs

- Recruiter message
- Company process file
- Candidate profile
- Writing style
- Desired outcome

## Process

1. Identify the situation:
   - scheduling
   - follow-up
   - compensation
   - offer clarification
   - interview confirmation
   - withdrawal
   - thank-you note
   - delay politely
2. Check existing process notes to avoid contradictions.
3. Draft 1-3 versions if tone is delicate.
4. Keep the message concise unless details are necessary.
5. Save the draft.
6. Update recruiter-messages and next actions.

## Output path

```text
outputs/drafts/<date>-<company>-<purpose>.md
```

## Tone rules

- Warm, professional, human.
- No unnecessary apologies.
- No defensive explanations.
- No over-disclosure of competing offers unless strategic and approved.
- If buying time, use legitimate administrative reasons when true, such as accountant review, schedule coordination, or document review.

## Useful patterns

### Delay while staying positive

`Everything sounds good on my side. Before I complete the signature, I’m just having my accountant review the tax setup I would need as an independent contractor, simply to make sure I handle everything correctly.`

### Ask for clarification

`Just to confirm, when you mention X, should I interpret that as Y, or is there a different practical setup for contractors?`

### Compensation adjustment

`After taking the contractor setup into account, I’d feel more comfortable around X. That said, the number is not fixed on my side, and I’d be happy to discuss it further depending on the overall setup.`

### Withdraw politely

`After giving it careful thought, I’ve decided to withdraw from the process at this time. I really appreciate your time and support throughout the process.`
