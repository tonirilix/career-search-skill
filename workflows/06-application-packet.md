# Workflow: Application Packet

## Goal

Create all materials needed to apply to a role without generic writing or false claims.

## Inputs

- Role analysis
- Company research
- Candidate profile
- Resume
- Writing style
- Compensation profile
- Relevant stories

## Output folder

```text
workspace/outputs/application-packets/<company>/
```

## Files to create

- `cover-letter.md`
- `form-answers.md`
- `positioning-notes.md`
- `resume-tailoring-notes.md`
- `submission-checklist.md`

## Cover letter rules

- Lead with specific role/company motivation.
- Connect candidate strengths to role requirements.
- Avoid generic `I am excited to apply...` unless a formal tone is requested.
- Keep it believable and not too long.
- Do not overclaim missing skills.

## Form answer rules

For each answer:

- Respect character limits.
- Provide 2-4 variants if tone/length is uncertain.
- Keep answers specific and human.
- Avoid generic company flattery.

Common questions:

- Why this company?
- Why this role?
- Tell us about yourself.
- What are your salary expectations?
- Describe your AI experience.
- What is your biggest career win?
- What is a hard technical decision you made?

## Positioning notes

Always include:

- Lead with:
- Secondary angle:
- Avoid overemphasizing:
- Compensation strategy:
- Possible concerns:
- Interview stories to use:

## Checklist

Include:

- Resume selected
- Cover letter ready
- Form answers ready
- Salary answer ready
- Application URL verified
- Submission status
- Follow-up date
