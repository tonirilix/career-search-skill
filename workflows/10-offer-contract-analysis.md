# Workflow: Offer and Contract Analysis

## Goal

Help the candidate understand practical tradeoffs in an offer or contractor agreement.

## Inputs

- Offer letter
- Contract text
- Recruiter messages
- Compensation profile
- Candidate preferences
- Competing offers/processes

## Output paths

```text
data/companies/<company>/offer.md
outputs/reports/<company>-offer-analysis.md
```

## Analyze

- Compensation
- Bonus
- Benefits
- PTO/sick time
- Employment model
- Tax/admin implications
- Payment terms
- Equipment
- Start date
- Termination terms
- Non-solicitation / non-compete
- IP assignment
- Arbitration / governing law
- Indemnification
- Contractor classification risk
- Mismatch between recruiter message and contract

## Categorize each item

- Standard
- Needs clarification
- Meaningful risk
- High risk
- Dealbreaker

## Important rule

Do not provide legal or tax advice as certainty. Provide practical analysis and recommend professional review for legal/tax decisions.

## Output sections

- Executive summary
- Money comparison
- Contract/practical risks
- Questions to ask
- Negotiation angle
- Recommendation

## Example clarification question

`Just to confirm, when you mention that contractors have access to the unlimited PTO policy, should I interpret that as unpaid time off handled under the same general team policy, or is there any paid component for contractors?`
