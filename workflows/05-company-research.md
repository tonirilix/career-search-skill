# Workflow: Company Research

## Goal

Give the candidate enough company context to decide, apply, and interview intelligently.

## Inputs

- Company name
- Role
- Company website
- News/articles/podcasts/investor pages, if web access exists
- Recruiter/employee messages

## Research questions

Answer:

- What does the company do?
- Who are its users/customers?
- What problem does it solve?
- What stage is the company in?
- What is the business model?
- What is the product/technical complexity?
- What does the company seem to value culturally?
- Any recent funding, layoffs, pivots, or product launches?
- Does it look like a product company, consultancy, agency, or unclear?
- What should the candidate emphasize in interviews?
- What should the candidate be cautious about?

## Output

Save to:

```text
data/companies/<company>/company-research.md
```

## Verification rules

- Current company facts require current sources if web access exists.
- Mark unsupported claims as inferred.
- Do not rely only on marketing pages when assessing risk.
- Preserve recruiter-provided context separately from public research.
